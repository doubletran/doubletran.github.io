Featured Projects

VR Pump Bearing Replacement (Unreal Engine)

I developed an immersive and interactive Virtual Reality (VR) experience that allows users to learn how to inspect, assemble, and replace ball bearings in a mechanical workshop environment.

Procedural Environment Creation (CityEngine, Unreal Engine)

For my capstone project, I built a 3D virtual environment in Unreal Engine from real data. The chosen model area is in Corvallis, Oregon, selected for its diverse landscapes —including natural areas (grasslands, woodlands, scrub), land uses (farmland, farmyards), and suburban housing. The ultimate goal is to showcase the integration workflow as a case study of the integration of GIS and Graphics to motivate further research and use cases in building the digital twin.

Planet Generator (OpenGL)

In this project, I built a planet generator using tetrahedron subdivision algorithm to generate terrain on spherical surfaces. Then, I applied a simplified model of biome classtification to assign dynamic color based on the biome (tundra, hot/cold dessert, forest,...) to simulate how natural conditions shape our environment.

Ocean Visualization On Globe (ArcGIS Pro, OpenGL Shader)

In this project, I used 2D Line Integral Convolution (LIC) technique to visualize ocean currents and temperature gradients on a 3D sphere. For the ocean data, I utilized ArcGIS Pro to extract and preprocess oceanographic datasets, applying geoprocessing tools to convert ocean flow and temperature data into color-encoded textures that can be read into the OpenGL shader.
